# StarBirdParaDox

A calm, personal-first family command center with an optional rough-day recovery path. Phase one gives you a place to land, somewhere to unload a crowded brain, and a few small ways to get through the next moment—without routines to fill in, streaks to keep, or chores to catch up on.

The optional companion, private wins, tiny universe, focus timers, thought bubbles, and Play Break activities are all extras; turn any of them off in Vibe Settings. Nothing is lost for skipping a game, quest, timer, or day.

## Run locally

Requires Node.js 18 or newer. No packages or external services are needed.

```sh
npm start
```

Open [http://localhost:4173](http://localhost:4173). Stop the server with `Ctrl+C`.

Run the checks with:

```sh
npm test
npm run check
```

Tasks, appearance, companion, rewards, and game progress are stored in this browser's local storage on this device. They are not uploaded or synced. Clearing browser data removes them. This repository has no accounts, authentication, calendar integration, analytics, or third-party services, so preferences are not associated with a sign-in and no real upcoming commitment can be displayed. Brain Dump's calendar option saves a clearly labeled local note only; it never creates a calendar event.

## Product direction

- **Phase 1 — Find your footing:** choose between a gentle reset, an anything-goes brain dump, or one tiny next step; keep loose tasks and a few priorities; use an optional Focus Toolkit, companion, private wins shelf, small local games, and a tiny universe. No streaks or penalties; adjust themes, motion, and extras in Vibe Settings. This is supportive organization, not clinical or emergency care.
- **Phase 2 — Shape your routines:** personal routines and gentle reminders, designed to be configurable and easy to pause.
- **Phase 3 — Coordinate a household:** optional household members, shared chores, and family coordination with clear consent and control over what is shared.

Later phases are direction, not features in this release. The app is supportive organization, not clinical care or emergency support. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency or crisis services.

## Fern & Cedar concept prototype

The [Fern & Cedar creative reuse and materials rescue concept](http://localhost:4173/fern-cedar/) is a separate static prototype for a proposed Bellingham / Whatcom County business. It includes local positioning, organizational options, and a launch checklist. The prototype is not an operating store, nonprofit, or active collection program.

The checklist stores progress in the browser's local storage on this device. The Fern & Cedar prototype has no backend, donation intake, checkout, or contact form. Its organizational guidance is not legal or tax advice, and its linked resources are starting points rather than evidence of local demand.
