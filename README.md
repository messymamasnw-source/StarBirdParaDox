# Fern & Cedar Creative Reuse

A responsive static concept prototype for a proposed creative reuse and materials rescue business in Bellingham, Washington, serving Whatcom County. It includes a business overview, a research-informed local position, organizational options, and an interactive micro-launch checklist.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve this directory with any static file server, for example:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8000`.

## Prototype notes

- This is a concept, not an operating store, nonprofit, or active collection program. The name and final public-facing brand remain provisional.
- Research links point to published organization and government resources. They are starting points, not proof of local demand or a complete competitive analysis. Validate the positioning through local conversations and a small pilot.
- The launch checklist saves progress in the current browser using local storage. There is no backend, active donation form, online checkout, or contact form.
- Materials should not be accepted without safety and condition review. Organizational options are planning prompts, not legal or tax advice. Do not promise tax-deductible donations without a qualified legal basis.
